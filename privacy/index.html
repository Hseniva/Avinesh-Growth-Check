Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains how Avinesh Bundhoo, trading as Hseniva (Arcabrite Ltd, Mauritius), collects and uses personal data through avineshbundhoo.com, hsenivamedia.com, the Avinesh Growth Check, the Digital Marketing Mastery onboarding form and our WhatsApp Business assistant.

1. What we collect

  • Forms: your name, business name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, website and the answers you give (for example your goals, budget range or learning preferences).
  • WhatsApp: your WhatsApp profile name, phone number and the messages you send to our business number, plus our replies.
  • Basic technical data: the page you used and the time of your submission.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you the recommendation, proposal, audit or learning plan you asked for.
  • To deliver our consulting, ads management and training services.
  • To keep a record of our conversations so we can follow up properly.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

3. Automated replies

Messages sent to our WhatsApp Business number may be answered first by an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) that follows our instructions. It can share information about our services and prices and will pass your request to Avinesh when a personal reply is needed. You can always ask to speak to a person.

4. Who processes your data

We use trusted service providers to run these tools, under their own security and privacy terms:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform)
  • Anthropic (AI assistant that drafts WhatsApp replies)
  • Google (Google Sheets, Gmail, Apps Script for storing form answers and notifications; Google Analytics for site measurement)
  • Meta Platforms (Meta Pixel, to measure ads, only with your consent)
  • n8n (workflow automation) and Render (website hosting)

Some of these providers process data outside Mauritius.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

On the Avinesh Growth Check and avineshbundhoo.com, I use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to measure visits, see which ads bring enquiries and improve my pages. These tools only set cookies if you click “Accept” on the cookie banner. If you reject, no analytics or advertising cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookies” link at the bottom of the Growth Check page.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and conversation records for up to 24 months after our last contact, or longer if needed for an active contract, invoicing or legal obligations.

7. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time. To request deletion, email digitalmarketer@hseniva.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and the phone number or email you used. We will confirm deletion within 30 days. You can also stop WhatsApp messages at any time by replying STOP or blocking our number.
