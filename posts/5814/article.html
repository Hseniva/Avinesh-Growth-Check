What is Google AI Max for Search?

AI Max for Search is a one-click bundle of AI features for standard Search campaigns. Google first announced it in beta in May 2025.

Instead of matching ads only to the keywords you typed in, it uses Google's AI to read the intent behind a search, your landing pages and your existing ads. Then it decides which queries to show for, what the ad should say and which page to send people to.

Your campaign structure, keywords and negative keywords stay in place. That is the big difference from handing everything to a black box.

The core features of AI Max

1. Search term matching

AI Max combines broad match and keywordless technology to find relevant searches your keyword list misses. Your negatives and brand exclusions still apply, so you keep control of where you don't want to appear.

2. Text customisation

Google generates headlines and descriptions from your website, existing ads and the user's query. It keeps testing variations and leans into what converts.

3. Final URL expansion

Rather than sending every click to one fixed landing page, AI Max can choose the most relevant page on your site for each search. A mismatched landing page is one of the most common reasons I see for a high CPA, so this matters.

4. Controls: brand, location and the AI Brief

You can add brand inclusions and exclusions, plus locations of interest at ad group level. That lets a UK travel brand, for example, reach people researching holidays in Mauritius, wherever they are searching from.

On 30 April 2026, Google also announced the AI Brief, a Gemini-powered set of plain-English instructions covering what your ads should say, which searches to prioritise or avoid, and which audiences get which messages. Existing text guidelines move into it, and it is rolling out in English.

How much does AI Max actually improve results?

Google's figures have changed as the product matured, so it is worth being precise:

  • Beta tests (2025): Google said advertisers typically saw 14% more conversions or conversion value at a similar CPA or ROAS, rising to 27% for campaigns built mostly on exact and phrase match.
  • General availability (2026): For campaigns already using search term matching, adding text customisation and final URL expansion delivers an average of 7% more conversions or conversion value, based on Google internal data for non-retail advertisers.

These are averages reported by Google, not guarantees. In my experience, results depend heavily on conversion tracking quality and how well your site is structured.

The AI Max timeline: what changed and what is coming

  • April 2026: AI Max for Search left beta and Google announced the move away from legacy formats.
  • June 2026: After advertiser feedback, Google delayed the DSA migration from September 2026 to February 2027. AI Max also became the default for new Search campaigns.
  • 3 August 2026: New campaigns could no longer be created with automatically created assets or campaign-level broad match.
  • 1 to 30 September 2026: Campaigns using those settings were automatically upgraded to AI Max.
  • January 2027: New Dynamic Search Ads campaigns can no longer be created.
  • February 2027: Remaining DSA campaigns migrate to AI Max automatically.

The 17 August bidding change you should check

From 17 August 2026, budget-limited campaigns using Target CPA or Target ROAS started delivering closer to their stated targets. If your campaign was beating a loose target, costs may have crept up towards it.

Google added a Bid Target Adjustment tool on 6 July 2026. If you haven't reviewed your targets since August, do it this week.

AI Max vs Performance Max: which should you use?

Performance Max runs across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Discover from one campaign. It is built for full-funnel reach, but you get less visibility into individual queries.

AI Max stays inside Search. You keep keywords, negatives, search term reports and ad group structure.

My rule of thumb: use AI Max for lead generation, B2B and high-intent direct response. Use Performance Max for ecommerce and broader reach. Many accounts benefit from both.

How to set up AI Max the right way: 6 steps

Step 1: Fix your conversion tracking first

AI Max optimises towards whatever you count as a conversion. If tracking is broken or full of low-value actions, the AI will chase the wrong thing. Strong first-party data gives it better signals.

Step 2: Check what was upgraded in September

Open each Search campaign and see whether AI Max is now on. Review the search terms and landing pages it has used since then, and add negatives where needed.

Step 3: Start with your best campaign

Switch AI Max on in a high-volume, well-converting campaign. Give it two to three weeks before judging, and compare against the previous period at a similar budget.

Step 4: Write your AI Brief and brand controls

Tell Google what to say, what never to say and which searches to avoid. Add brand exclusions and URL exclusions for pages like careers or old promotions.

Step 5: Clean up your landing pages

Final URL expansion can only pick good pages if you have them. Make sure key service and product pages are clear, current and indexed. The same work helps your visibility in AI search engines.

Step 6: Plan your DSA migration before February 2027

List every Dynamic Search Ads campaign, map it to an AI Max equivalent and use Google's one-click upgrade on your own schedule. Migrating voluntarily lets you test and adjust before the forced switch. If you're curious where this is heading, read my take on AI agents in campaign management.

Frequently asked questions

Is AI Max a new campaign type?

No. AI Max is a set of AI features you switch on inside a standard Search campaign. Your keywords, negatives and ad groups stay in place.

When do Dynamic Search Ads stop?

New DSA campaigns can't be created from January 2027, and remaining DSA campaigns are migrated to AI Max automatically in February 2027. Google originally planned this for September 2026 but delayed it.

How much uplift can I expect from AI Max?

Google reports an average of 7% more conversions or conversion value at a similar CPA or ROAS when text customisation and final URL expansion are added to search term matching. Your results depend on tracking quality and landing pages.

Should I use AI Max or Performance Max?

Use AI Max when you want Search-only reach with keyword control and query reporting, such as lead generation. Use Performance Max for cross-channel ecommerce and reach. Many accounts run both.

Conclusion

AI Max is no longer optional. It is the default for new Search campaigns, and Dynamic Search Ads have a fixed end date.
